# Echo Protocol

[Download the macOS playtest on itch.io](https://lautigr116.itch.io/echo-protocol).
La embestida / Incursion, packaged 2026-10-19 for Intel and Apple Silicon.
This is an in-development downloadable game; see
[release scope and checks](Docs/RELEASE_20261019.md). Day-to-day development
continues in the Editor; this publication was explicitly requested.

Echo Protocol is a first-person psychological horror and temporal puzzle game
about cooperating with recordings of your own past actions. The mechanic begins
as a reliable puzzle tool and later becomes the source of uncertainty.

This is a solo university project targeting a short, polished 30–45 minute game.
The greybox first-person movement and center-camera interaction checkpoints are
human-approved. Mouse sensitivity remains `0.15`. The first temporal recording,
reset, and Echo playback checkpoint is also human-approved. The first
pressure-plate/door puzzle and its timing are now human-approved. M5, normal
puzzle language, is complete: M5.1 and M5.2 are human-approved. M6.1 now adds one
objectively verified audiovisual discrepancy without changing Echo playback or
puzzle rules. The visual-only pass was noticed but did not create discomfort;
the clinical hum and its disappearance are now human-approved. M6.2 adds the
first objectively verified 3D door-audio source. Its causal timing is
human-approved, while spatial localization was not tested separately. AD2 has
now replaced its synthetic motor with a user-selected domestic sliding-door
recording and matching endpoint; the integrated route is human-approved for
continued prototyping while every timbre remains replaceable. M6.3 now adds one
objectively verified,
proximity-triggered chair sound and has passed a guided human one-shot check.
That pass produced no terror or discomfort, so spontaneous attribution,
localization, and emotional effectiveness remain open. The chair is therefore a
secondary environmental proof, not the project's headline scare. AV1 gives the
same mechanical room an objectively verified clinical visual foundation without
changing puzzle geometry, Echo behavior, or audio. Its human visual checkpoint is
approved at prototype level.

## Current controls

- Move: `WASD`
- Look: mouse
- Interact: `E`
- Finish/reset the current attempt: `R`
- Pause / resume: `Escape`
- Optional help: `H`
- Capture cursor again: left click in the Game view

Recording attempts reset automatically after 60 seconds. In the campaign house,
recording is explicitly suspended; chapter continuation restores an origin, not
an old recording.
The recorder remains armed while the Player is idle at spawn. The first real
movement starts the timeline with a fixed one-second lead-in; every later pause,
including holding a pressure plate, is reproduced at full length.

## Historical development checkpoint

The old plan to stretch the current two-minute puzzle into a 5–8 minute route
around the chair is superseded. The project is now horror-first: sound is the
primary perceptual weapon, puzzles create exposure and divided attention, and
the 30–45 minute experience will be shaped as an escalation rather than padded
with disconnected plate rooms. Echo playback remains exact throughout.

SD1 is now frozen as a partial technical sandbox: its authored triggers, moving
world source, routing, and provisional level are reusable evidence, but its room
and cue sequence are not production content and did not validate a complete fear
beat. The rejected breathing remains removed and the reserved whisper remains
unimplemented.

The current milestone is `EM1 / VS1-R3 Hub Return`.
R2/P3 are now **creator-approved at greybox level**, as confirmed in the R3 brief.
R1's visual/spatial direction and legibility are creator-approved; its emotional
recovery effect remains **EN PRUEBA**. P1, SE1 and P2 retain their approved greybox
checkpoints; SE2/SE3 retain their approved direction and slice legibility.

R3 is implemented and objectively verified. A real Player crossing beyond the
P3 final gate, with Echo A authorization and a genuine live B hold, unlocks a
physical western return into the **original clinical Hub**, arriving from its
previously inaccessible west side. The exit stays unlocked through R; R still
returns to R1 and resets P3 normally. Two automatic solid arrival leaves prevent
reverse entry from the Hub. One distant static curtain remains behind sealed
glass; no new puzzle, checkpoint, actor, audio or strong event is added.

The final regression covers the domestic checkpoint → P2 → SE2 → SE3 → R1 → E →
P3 → Hub route, invalid solutions, actor rejection, five subsequent RECALLs and
arrival retreat/reverse attempts. Geometry passes 18,328 floor samples and six
capsule-grid connectivity cases. All 28 pre-existing gameplay scripts and 29
reset references are preserved. Full P1/SE1 playback was not repeated. Spatial
recognition, corridor duration, tension, interpretation and emotional effect remain
**EN PRUEBA**. See [R3 evidence and human test](Docs/VisualEvidence/2026-09-12-R3/README.md).

After the genuine SE3 return opens R1, the central station accepts the existing
**E** interaction. Its stable cyan display confirms the new R1 RECALL origin and
opens the north transition into **Return Gallery**. Activation clears obsolete
recording history and rearms immediately, without moving the Player or invoking
RECALL. Future R returns to R1; the Echo records only actual position and yaw.

Beyond the quiet room, P3 recombines a latched Echo authorization at Reader A
with a live directional hold at Reader B. The Player follows an alternative
route behind an opaque partition, then crosses the final gate while the Echo
holds B. The payoff is a 13.4 m clinical gallery with sealed glazing, a distant
view of the actual R1 station, and one restrained textile remnant. It prepares
Denial without adding a strong event or audio.

Implemented and objectively verified in Unity: existing E activation, the real
P2 → SE2 → SE3 → R1 route from a prepared domestic checkpoint, P3 solve and
negative cases, 12 input-driven RECALLs, immutable Echo poses, 6,402 floor samples,
boundaries and capsule-sized route checks. P1/SE1 remain configured and preserved;
their complete opening sequence was not replayed in this test. Unprimed-player clarity, tension,
orientation, timing comfort and R1 recovery remain **EN PRUEBA**.
See the concrete [human playtest](Docs/ROADMAP.md#r2-human-playtest) and
[objective checks](Docs/VisualEvidence/2026-09-12-R2/positive.checks.txt).

this corrected causal response in Play Mode. VT1 now gives this preserved
blockout a restrained production-intent target: a cooler clinical/service
palette, a warm domestic threshold with console/radiator/curtain/runner
silhouettes, and one scene-local post-processing profile. The three P1 readers

remains unobstructed.

pass and now accepts the door sound when attributed to the modeled door. Strong
Event 1 is implemented as an objective prototype: after P1 and the end of the
Echo, a domestic knock requires a visible check of the empty lane. As soon as
the `0.35 s` look is registered and the knock has ended, the facility now begins
extinguishing cumulatively from far to near at `z=4/9/14`; there is no additional

stage. The three failures

readable shape is the warm doorway plus its narrow spill along the Player route.
The sequence briefly
subtracts the two facility beds and restores them while the blackout and
threshold reveal persist. After `0.75 s` of darkness, the modeled domestic door
opens inward over `5.2 s`; its moving collider follows the leaf, a provisional
pitched domestic-friction loop plays from the hinge, and warm light grows only
with the aperture. Crossing the open threshold commits a new RECALL point inside
the domestic room. After `1.1 s`, the leaf slams shut over `0.18 s`, its fitted
collider seals the former route, and one spatial procedural impact plays from the
hinge. Later RECALLs return the Player to that interior point without replaying
the portazo. This crossing is the first slice checkpoint. RECALL
before the lane sequence now cancels the incomplete knock/check attempt and
rearms SE1 behind P1, avoiding a stranded intermediate phase after the Player
returns to spawn. Once the lane sequence begins, RECALL still resets P1 but does
not close the door or remove the environmental consequence. Startup silence,
three-stage ordering, single-fire behavior, `0.38`
audio duck/recovery, lighting persistence, threshold commit, closed-door
collision, repeated domestic RECALL placement, references, and zero gameplay
Console errors pass objective checks. This replaces
the human-rejected standalone footsteps without forced camera motion or unsafe
gain. The near-black warm invitation is human-approved. Portazo timbre, surprise,
timing, and comfort still need a human headphone pass. Final pacing, final
dressing, and production audio remain unapproved.

P2 Blind Alignment now extends the learned optical-reader rule inside the
domestic checkpoint. Correct Player orientation temporarily previews a north
gate opening; solving it requires a recorded Echo to remain aligned while the
Player crosses into a short L-shaped service return. The turn removes the Echo
from sight without adding a timer, a second sensor, or a new mechanic. Reader,
gate, relay, reset wiring, continuous floor support, orientation rejection,
Echo-only hold, blind-corner occlusion, and real-RECALL cleanup pass objective
checks. Committing the domestic checkpoint now also discards the route recorded
before the crossing and immediately rearms the recorder, so an immediate P2
RECALL produces an Echo whose first frame is inside the domestic sector. Dark
fitted jambs hide the service-wall reveals that previously read green beside the
gate. The regression ends with zero gameplay Console errors. The developer then
validated the corrected continuous-route solve, including the domestic-sector
Echo origin; P2 is human-approved at greybox level.

Strong Event 2's architectural replacement changes the inaccessible frontal
cold chamber into a deep domestic room after the genuine Echo-held P2 crossing
and visible reading. Completed SE2 persists through RECALL. SE3 then overlaps
four domestic frames and a runner with the clinical return on the real Player's
westward crossing; its revealed consequence also persists. The creator has
human-approved this direction and the legibility of the slice. Fear, exact
narrative meaning and final presentation remain separate evidence gates.

AD2 has downloaded and reviewed six CC0 recordings outside the project, retained
sanitized license evidence and hashes, and imported eight edited prototype clips.
It replaces the facility-air and door procedurals, adds fluorescent and distant
machinery beds, gives both plates truthful edge cues, and adds successful-RECALL
feedback. Objective wiring/reset checks pass. Only the domestic door A/B pair is
a strong initial direction; the integrated route is now human-approved for
continued prototyping. Every imported clip, including the door pair, remains
explicitly replaceable and no production timbre is locked.

M6.3 remains objectively verified but emotionally open. Its chair and cue may
remain as a secondary motif or technical reference, but they no longer define
the next horror milestone. The cue will not be duplicated or made louder to
create impact cheaply.

AV1 adds a closed ceiling, clinical wall/floor separation, restrained fixtures,
dark trim, and a muted domestic material contrast on the chair. It is still
prototype presentation: there are no textures, fog, post-processing, production
props, or new horror event. Five consecutive RECALLs and the known puzzle
baseline passed after the visual change, and the developer approved the visual
foundation for continued prototyping.
