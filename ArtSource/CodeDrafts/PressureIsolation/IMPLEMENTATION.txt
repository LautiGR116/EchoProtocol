PLAYER CONTRACT

One hand-operated isolation control at the existing first pump, approximately
(5.9,1.5,-36.5), approached at x4.5. The center ray starts the interaction;
holding E rotates its visible lever through 70 degrees over 1.8 seconds.
The Player remains free to look around. There is no requirement to keep the
control inside the camera frame, no HUD progress bar, and no unseen gaze timer.
Physical proximity and an unobstructed reach to gripPoint remain necessary.

Before the detent, releasing E or moving out of reach releases the mechanism;
it springs back over .45 seconds. A fresh press is needed to grab again once
it has returned. At the detent the lever stays at its new stop. The Player can
release E immediately and walk. This is one continuous manual operation.

The manual assembly cuts the motor and its service work lights. The second
assembly is an automatic pressure relief: different silhouette/guard, no E
prompt, two gauges showing the same real pressure. The automatic outlet hisses
locally while pressure falls for about three seconds; the motor coasts down
over 2.2 seconds. Zero pressure releases the south bulkhead. No remote manual
wheel secretly rotates and no second control must be activated.

Both work lights go out together; retain authored emergency/residual light,
floor edges and the exit's small pilot. Do not repeat SE1's sequential blackout.
After A the route passes along the eastern side of the opaque service cabinet
and turns south toward the door at z=-48, roughly 13 metres of normal walking.
The pressure falls during that movement. The Player's footsteps and the real
door remain audible in the stopped machinery. No new scare, phantom source,
camera motion or manufactured Echo action is part of this change.

Two short physical labels are enough: A "AISLAR / MANTENER E", positions
"MARCHA / SERVICIO"; B "PURGA AUTOMÁTICA". The gauge, falling motor, stable
lever and pressure door establish causality. House supply, telephone behavior,
reserve, key and choices remain unchanged.

PAUSE, RECALL, CONTINUE

The component progresses the hand in LateUpdate, after PlayerInteractor can
open a document. Pausing cancels grip and requires release before a fresh E.
While paused nothing moves, drains or commits. On resume a released control
returns visibly to rest; an E still held cannot restart it. Once latched, pause
freezes the genuine coast-down/drain and resumes that consequence normally.

R stays inert from the real R3 boundary through the plant and house. LastRecording
remains the exact P3 object. No CompleteLoop, SetRecallCheckpoint, invented frames,
new Echo or live ResetPose occurs in this stretch. The component also guards R
after a fresh Continue, where R3's transient boundary never fired. That guard
releases only when a later real checkpoint changes CheckpointRevision; normal
BeginReview at 6B does exactly that. Final 6B recording/witness rules are untouched.

Use a fresh checkpointKey for the new edition. The Director persists only the
chapter and one extra bit, checkpointKey + ".isolated". Save once on physical
entry and once on actual detent, honoring SuppressSaves. Never serialize handle
fraction, draining seconds, current pressure, audio cursor or a recording.

Continue Pressure before detent => lever MARCHA, pressure1, motor1, door closed.
Continue Pressure after detent => lever at stop, pressure0, motor0, door open.
Both place the Player at the explicit safe entrance; no shutdown performance
replays. House/Archive imply completed isolation and restore that settled state.
Existing domestic answered/light fields and genuine 6B origin rules remain.
New mode never saves or accepts the removed Gallery chapter. RestoreState does
not increment commit/drain counters or emit their events; RestoreRevision informs
audio to rebaseline without a latch/purge cue. Restore is a recovery fixture/API,
not evidence that the Player performed the action in that run.

Recommended key: EP.CampaignCheckpoint.Isolation.v1. Keep it on the new scene's
Director only; no migration or deletion of saved chapters from older editions.
PrepareHouseProbe explicitly restores isolated state in this opt-in mode so
older isolated house/final fixtures remain usable and honestly labelled.
