## Five-chapter route

### 1. Protocol — 5–6 minutes

- **Route:** Entry, Hub, first half of the Glass Test Lane, Hub.
- **Purpose:** teach movement, recording, RECALL, predictable Echo completion,
  truthful mechanism audio, and reset.
- **Mechanic:** one body-presence calibration with generous timing; it is
  onboarding, not another plate-room puzzle.
- **Vulnerability:** the Player briefly faces a diagnostic surface with the room
  behind them, but no threat is confirmed.
- **Horror pressure:** coherent facility sounds and one ambiguous structural
  impact. No domestic motif and no strong event.
- **Recovery:** the Hub is unchanged and audibly stable.

### 2. Familiarity — 6–7 minutes

- **Route:** Hub, complete Glass Test Lane, parallel Service Return, known Hub.
- **Puzzle P1 — Parallel Observation Relay:** the Player records an Echo walking
  the glass-side lane and facing two optical readers. During RECALL, those
  readers authorize doors in the parallel service passage. Position and yaw are
  sufficient; hidden timing, frame-tight execution, and semantic replay are not.
- **Vulnerability:** the Player loses sight of the Echo behind partitions and
  must wait at one service reader while facing away from the glass.
- **Motif:** a dry household knock prompts a check that initially yields nothing.
- **Strong event 1 — Occupied lane:** only after the Echo ends, a second set of
  footsteps moves through the visibly empty lane toward a newly visible warm
  domestic threshold. It is environmental presence, not reproduced Echo audio.
- **Consequence:** the threshold remains visible on the return. The Player may
  inspect it through glass but cannot enter yet.
- **Recovery:** stable Hub bed returns with one domestic detail left in view.

### 3. Recollection — 7–8 minutes

- **Route:** familiar Hub and lane, newly opened Domestic Insert, Blind Service
  Return.
- **Puzzle P2 — Blind Alignment:** from the committed interior checkpoint,
  extend the learned optical-reader language so the Echo must hold a readable
  orientation while the Player crosses the north gate into the return. The
  Player chooses when to separate; the rule remains deterministic.
- **Vulnerability:** leaving the warm space requires abandoning the Echo facing
  the domestic reader, then turning a corner that removes it from sight.
- **Motifs:** household door, fabric or furniture weight, and an incomplete
  melodic fragment. Avoid intimate breathing unless a distinctly non-erotic
  performance is later human-approved.
- **Strong event 2 — Impossible domestic chamber:** the first side-window
  figure study failed human detection and is preserved disabled. Its implemented
  replacement puts an inaccessible cold service chamber directly ahead after
  the P2 turn, then reveals a deeper amber domestic interior and a clear,
  stationary human silhouette. The environmental change leaves the Echo and its
  renderer untouched and adds no audio or forced camera movement. A live P2 Echo
  hold authorizes the Player crossing; `0.65 s` of clear natural view triggers
  the reveal, followed by `3.2 s` of actual visible reading before completion.
  Input-driven route, gating, reading-time and RECALL checks pass. The creator
  approved direction and slice legibility; emotional effect remains unverified.
- **Consequence:** incomplete RECALL restores the cold chamber for a retry;
  completion keeps the warm architecture and figure stable through RECALL for
  the Play session. An accessible physical memory fragment remains planned,
  not implemented; its exact sibling/child meaning is TBD.
- **Recovery:** implemented R1 provides a wider clinical observation room with
  stable cool lighting and distant static domestic residue. It adds no music or
  close presence; its recovery effect remains a human test.

### 4. Denial — 7–8 minutes

- **Route:** altered Hub, Service Return, lane seen from the opposite side, long
  return through known geometry.
- **Mechanic:** recombine P1 and P2 with larger spatial separation, not tighter
  timing. The Player commits to RECALL before entering a route where the Echo
  cannot be monitored continuously.
- **Vulnerability:** sustained separation, a required wait, and a return through
  a corridor whose acoustic identity is already learned.
- **Motifs:** facility strain, presence following outside the reachable route,
  and an authored time/repetition variant. Functional cues remain truthful.
- **Strong event 3 — Omitted passage:** physical environmental evidence implies
  that the protagonist crossed the service route in an omitted cycle. The
  current recording and Echo remain exact; no known log or captured frame is
  altered.
- **Consequence:** the familiar return opens onto an impossible overlap of Hub
  and Domestic Insert rather than a disconnected new room.
- **Recovery:** brief recognizable Hub composition, damaged but quiet.

### 5. Acceptance — 5–6 minutes

- **Route:** merged Hub/lane/insert, then a final direct path.
- **Mechanic:** one forgiving recombination of the learned relay. It demonstrates
  trust in the Echo rather than introducing a final puzzle rule.
- **Vulnerability:** the Player deliberately summons the exact Echo into the
  most emotionally contaminated version of its familiar route.
- **Strong event 4 — Convergence:** facility and domestic motifs resolve into the
  confrontation with both connected traumas. Exact narrative staging is TBD;
  there is no boss, combat, or generic chase.
- **Consequence:** the protagonist confronts the memory. Final C ends the
  nightmare and cuts to black without showing whether they wake.
- **Recovery:** emotional deceleration and silence before the cut, not another
  sting.

## Motif and consequence discipline

| Family | Protocol | Familiarity | Recollection | Denial | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Facility / structure | Establish causality | Distant activity | Contaminated baseline | Sustained strain | Resolves or falls away |
| Presence / body | None confirmed | Footsteps beyond glass | Figure/reflection | Following outside route | Human meaning, not enemy reveal |
| Domestic memory | Absent | Knock and warm threshold | Material and incomplete melody | Invades navigation | Coexists with facility |
| Time / repetition | Exact Echo baseline | Same route remains readable | Exact path gains new context | Omitted-cycle evidence | Exact Echo becomes chosen support |

Use the cadence `hear → locate → check → find nothing → continue → partial
evidence` selectively. Strong events 1–4 receive persistent consequences. Minor
cues sometimes receive none so the sequence does not become predictable.

  Echo rule.
- The return route is recognized before its meaning changes.

  comparable perceptual response occurs; deterministic cue delivery alone does

- The transition reads as institution invaded by domestic memory, not as two
  unrelated asset sets.

  preserve essential information. Summed output has measured headroom.
- A target-hardware profile establishes the first evidence-based performance
