# Echo Protocol — credits

Design and development: Echo Protocol project (individual student production).
Production assistance: OpenAI Codex. Environment models, layout and procedural
relay/door effects were created for this project. No new paid service or asset
package was used for this edition.

(§2.5, §4.1(b)); standalone redistribution of raw or derived 3D data is excluded
(§4.3(b)). AI/ML restrictions remain applicable. No provider account, purchase,
voice cloning, paid generation, or third-party AI upload was used.

Original project work retargets Nathan's walk to Eric, closes an in-place cycle,
authors quiet/grip/present postures, foot placement and captured facial pulses,
and configures URP body/eye materials. Provenance, original URLs, hashes and

prefab remain in excluded local folders; they are included only in the personal
recovery archive and the permitted compiled game. Public source is intentionally
insufficient to recreate this licensed candidate without obtaining the models.

matches the Echo's cotton shirt. Existing traced audio and fonts are retained.
